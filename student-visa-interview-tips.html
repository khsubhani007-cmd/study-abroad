Student Visa Interview Tips: How to Answer With Confidence

Practical student visa interview tips for Pakistani students: common questions, how to explain your choice of course and country, and mistakes that lead to refusals.

Study Abroad By SiU TeamStudy abroad counsellors, Lahore📅 18 Oct 2026⏱ 5 min read

⭐ Key takeaways

  • Know your course, university, costs and career plan in your own words.
  • Be honest and consistent with your documents.
  • Practise speaking clearly — confidence comes from preparation.
In this guide
  1. Questions you should expect
  2. How to answer well
  3. Mistakes to avoid
  4. FAQ

Many study visa applications include an interview or a credibility check. The officer wants to know you're a genuine student with a clear plan.

Questions you should expect

  • Why this course and this university?
  • Why this country instead of studying in Pakistan?
  • Who is funding your studies, and how?
  • What will you do after graduating?
  • Tell me about your previous studies or work.

How to answer well

  • Answer in your own words — don't memorise scripts
  • Give specific details about modules, city and costs
  • Keep answers short and clear
  • Make sure everything matches your documents
💡
Counselor tip

Practise with someone who will ask follow-up questions. The second question is where unprepared answers fall apart.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Not knowing your own course details
  • Unclear source of funds
  • Saying your main goal is to work
  • Answers that contradict your SOP

Frequently asked questions

Is there an interview for every student visa?
Not always. It depends on the country and the case, but you should always be ready to explain your plans.
Can I take the interview in Urdu?
Interviews are usually in English, and your English is often part of what's being assessed.
