Overview

Why students choose Cyprus

Cyprus is a member of the European Union with a relaxed Mediterranean lifestyle and many programmes taught in English. Its institutions accept students in both autumn and January/February intakes.

The key to a successful plan is choosing a properly accredited institution in the Republic of Cyprus — our counselors check this for every student.
